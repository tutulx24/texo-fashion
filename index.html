Who We Are

Over 15 years of industry leadership in sourcing, manufacturing, and global export operations.

We have developed unshakeable expertise across the entire apparel supply chain. Operating our own highly advanced knit composite garment factory alongside a strictly evaluated network of partner factories, we provide our international buyers with absolute flexibility, scalability, and unmatched comprehensive apparel solutions.

Composite Factory

Fully equipped, compliance-certified in-house manufacturing ensuring 100% control over production timelines and quality parameters.

Sourcing Partner

Leveraging vast networks to source the finest raw materials, fabrics, and accessories to bring your design concepts to reality.

Global Export

Seamless logistics and export management ensuring timely delivery of goods to North America, Europe, and Latin America.

Our Product Portfolio

Specializing in a massive range of high-quality fashion wear for Men, Women, and Kids.

Quality & Compliance

We don't just manufacture clothes; we ensure ethical practices and uncompromising quality standards.

Strict Quality Control (QMS)

Our dedicated QA team implements inline and final inspections (AQL standards) at every stage of production, guaranteeing that every piece shipped meets your exact specifications and tolerances.

Ethical Manufacturing

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for child labor and forced labor. Our facilities ensure safe working conditions, fair wages, and strict adherence to international labor laws and human rights.

On-Time Delivery

Time to market is critical in retail. Our proactive production planning, critical path management, and efficient supply chain logistics ensure 100% on-time shipment, every time.

Professional Support

From initial tech-pack review and sample development to final shipment, our merchandising team provides 24/7 transparent communication and professional business support.
